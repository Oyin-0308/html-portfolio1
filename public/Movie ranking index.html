The Best Movies According To Stephanie

My Top 3 Movies of all Time

The Old Guard

This is such a good movie. Its full of action.

Troy

This is one of my favourite movies. It has stunning images of the historical locations, beautiful blue seas, lovely costumes and makeup, great action scenes and beautiful looking people.

Shark Tale

This is such a fun movie. Wonderful animation, great visuals and funny.

The Pursuit of Happyness

This is such an inspiring movie. Very sad at times but also heart-warming. Very Poignant.
